Member Login

Your individual history and saved GPS routes are private to your account. The public leaderboard shares your display name, weekly and all-time distance, and walk counts. After the Supabase move, choose Create Account and set a new password with your mobile number; old passwords are not transferred. Older walk records are not linked to new accounts and will not appear in personal history. Mobile numbers are not SMS-verified, and password recovery requires help from an administrator.
